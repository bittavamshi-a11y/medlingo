About MEDLINGO

MEDLINGO is a student project that helps people describe a symptom in their own language (by typing or speaking), suggests the type of consultant doctor to see, and lists safe self-care steps. When an AI key is configured on the server, answers are generated by an AI model in the chosen language; otherwise built-in keyword rules are used.

♡
Important

MEDLINGO cannot diagnose or rule out any condition and gives no medicine doses. For severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, call your local emergency number (112 in India) or visit a hospital.
